When you are logged to bandit32, the starting shell
is uppershell with an -s bit. uppershell is owned
by bandit33, and once you break from it, it drops
you to bandit33 home directory with privileges of bandit33.

To break from uppershell, type $0. $0 is the name of
the starting shell (sh). Start bash from sh, and
the rest is simple:

cat /etc/bandit_pass/bandit33
